Education

I graduated from Brigham Young University in December 2023 with a BA in Editing and Publishing and minors in Linguistics Computing, Digital Humanities and Technology, and Philosophy. My overall GPA was 3.99. Here's some info about my degree and minors:

BA: Editing and Publishing

Editing and Publishing is a unique major which frames writing and editing as professional applications of linguistics, and it has helped me approach writing and editing with clarity, accuracy, and attention to detail. It also provided me with training on technical editing and a variety of genres of professional writing.

Minor: Linguistics Computing

Linguistics Computing is a minor focused on performing linguistic analysis with computational tools and techniques. Skills: Web scraping, corpus compilation and analysis, sentiment analysis, syntax analysis, etc.

Minor: Digital Humanities and Technology

Digital Humanities and Technology is a minor primarily comprising web development and design classes. Relevant skills I've learned in this minor are the Adobe Suite (especially InDesign); front-end web development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; and CRUD apps with MySQL and PHP.

Minor: Philosophy

Philosophy introduced me to a variety of philosophical thinkers and traditions, and it also introduced me to formal logic, which I found helpful in understanding programming.

Personal background

I've enjoyed reading and writing most of my life. In college, I chose to major in editing and publishing, which I hoped would guide me to a career in writing and editing.

At the same time, I was developing an interest in programming and web development. It appealed to the logic-driven part of my brain. Thanks to some classes in my program, I discovered technical writing. I enjoyed the process of learning about technical subjects and writing for smart people. The one thing that really stuck with me from my studies is that the most important part of writing is understanding your audience.

I became a full-time technical writer in 2023. Ever since, I've been enjoying the constant learning and problem solving that comes with working on software documentation.

In my free time, I like lifting weights, running, and playing soccer. I read, too. Primarily novels. Susan Sontag explains why it's important to read novels here.
